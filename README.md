# Robert Cantu

I build learning tools and practical AI systems that make complex topics easier to understand and use. My current work connects RHIT exam preparation, interactive learning, and responsible data governance.

## Projects in progress

### RHIT Rescue Grid
An 11-stage learning hub for RHIT exam preparation. I am developing interactive stations, applied practice, and a guided learning path. The source repository is private while I review the rights and provenance of study content and media.

### The Adventures of RHIT HIM Technician Certification Quest
A game-based RHIT learning project with a planned 29 Pro Levels. The current working build centers on Sphere 1, with reusable launch and interface scaffolding. Question banks and media are being reviewed before any public release.

### I.C.O.N.I.C. — Building H.A.V.E.N.
A developing reader guide and system architecture exploring data governance, AI integration, and the H.A.V.E.N., A.N.G.E.L., and H.A.Z.E.L. concepts. The Reader Edition is in a chapter-by-chapter review process; it is not a finished publication.

## What I am learning

I am practicing how to turn a large idea into a tested, documented project: mapping learning goals, building interfaces, keeping recovery checkpoints, and checking the rights and safety of material before sharing it.

The project repositories are private backups. This public profile gives an overview of my work without redistributing study questions, media, or draft manuscript pages.
